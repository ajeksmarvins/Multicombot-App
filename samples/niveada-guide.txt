ABOUT NIVEADA
NIVEADA provides business stationery and office supply services in Abuja, Nigeria.

OPENING HOURS
The office is open Monday to Friday, from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM West Africa Time. It is closed on Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays.

DELIVERY
Standard delivery within Abuja takes two to three business days after payment confirmation. Delivery outside Abuja is not currently offered.

RETURNS
Unused, unopened stationery may be returned within seven days of delivery with the original receipt. Custom-printed items cannot be returned unless defective.

CONTACT
For enquiries, use the contact form on this assistant. A company representative will review the enquiry. The form does not book appointments or send an automatic email.
